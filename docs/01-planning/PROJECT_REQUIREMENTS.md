# Project Requirements

## 1. Problem Statement

Mohammed Fahaman is a Full Stack Developer & AI Engineer specializing in modern web applications, the MERN stack, and generative AI tooling. He requires a production-grade, highly responsive, and authentic portfolio to showcase his real-world projects, verified certifications (Google Cloud, Scaler RAG Masterclass, Anthropic Claude prompting), academic achievements, and technical expertise to recruiters, clients, and collaborators worldwide.

## 2. Target Audience

- Technical recruiters and hiring managers looking for full-stack and AI software engineers.
- Engineering leads seeking developers with verified cloud, RAG pipeline, and modern frontend capabilities.
- Founders and organizations needing modern digital products and contract engineering services.
- Fellow developers exploring open-source projects and code craftsmanship.

## 3. Functional Requirements

- **Hero Presentation**: High-impact introduction, headline, quick social links, downloadable resume, and direct CTA.
- **Skills Matrix**: Categorized, responsive showcase of Frontend, Backend, Databases, Languages, Cloud & DevOps, AI & LLMs, and Tools.
- **Project Showcase**: Detailed case cards with tech stacks, problem tags, years, and direct repository links.
- **Certifications & Badges**: Verified Google Cloud skill badges, Scaler RAG masterclass certificates, Claude session credentials, and downloadable workshop certificates with hover previews.
- **Interactive Lightbox Gallery**: Keyboard-navigable (Escape, Arrow Left/Right) modal displaying authentic professional photos.
- **Direct Contact Channel**: Validated inquiry form dispatching messages instantly via EmailJS with visual feedback states.
- **Legal & Compliance Pages**: Real accessible pages for Privacy Policy (`/privacy`), Terms of Service (`/terms`), and Cookie Policy (`/cookies`).

## 4. Non-Functional Requirements

- **Performance**: 95+ Google Lighthouse scores; First Contentful Paint under 1.2s; SSR bundle optimization.
- **Accessibility**: WCAG 2.2 Level AA compliance; zero critical contrast defects; keyboard focus outlines.
- **Security**: No hardcoded API secrets or backend keys in client bundles; HTTPS enforcement; sanitized inputs.
- **Design Integrity**: Strict avoidance of generic pill buttons and purple gradients; refined dark/light mode palette.
